Talk instead of typing. In Arabic, in English, or in both within one sentence.

Navo is an open source voice keyboard for macOS. Hold one key, speak naturally, release, and Navo pastes clean text into the app you are working in. Speech recognition, summaries and your history all stay on your Mac.

Three moves, in any app

  1. Hold

    Hold Right Option wherever you can type. The Flow Bar opens with a live waveform, so you see that Navo hears you.

  2. Speak

    Talk the way you normally do. Arabic dialects, English, and switching between them in the middle of a sentence all work.

  3. Let go

    Navo tidies the text and pastes it where your cursor is. You do not copy, switch windows or press anything. Double tap the key to talk hands free, Esc cancels.

What Navo does for you

Dictation is where it starts. Navo also writes down your meetings, turns recordings into text, writes the summary and remembers what you copied. None of it leaves your Mac.

It pastes the text for you

Let go of the key and the sentence is already in your email. Navo puts the text on the clipboard, presses Command V in the app you are in, then puts back whatever you had copied before. You never switch windows and you never paste.

  • Works wherever a text field has focus: Mail, Slack, the browser, your code editor.
  • First a small model on your Mac fixes the punctuation and drops the filler words. It never translates and never changes your dialect.
  • No text field in focus? The text waits on the clipboard and Navo tells you. Password fields are never filled.
  • Every dictation stays on your Mac with its recording: search it, replay it, retry it.

Your meeting, written down while it happens

Record a Google Meet, a Zoom call or anything your Mac plays, your microphone, or both. Every minute or so the newest part turns into text, so you can read along while people are still talking.

  • The audio is only ever cut inside a pause, so no word is split in two.
  • Pause and resume. The recording is saved in parts, so a crash loses almost nothing.
  • The menu bar shows a red dot and the time while you record.

  • Gemma 4 or Llama 3.1 writes it on your Mac, and you watch the answer appear.

Any recording becomes text you can search

Drop in Voice Memos, WhatsApp voice notes, MP3, M4A, WAV or FLAC. Navo writes them out piece by piece, with times if you want them, ready to copy or export.

  • Search looks in the titles and inside the text. Arabic matches without diacritics or hamza forms.
  • Filter by date: today, yesterday, the last 7 or 30 days, or one day from the calendar.
  • Send audio in with Share in Voice Memos, a paste, a drop, or Open With in Finder.

Everything you copied, one shortcut away

  • Copied something on your iPhone? With Universal Clipboard on, it lands here too.
  • Star what you want to keep forever. The rest leaves after the time you choose, from 1 day to 2 months.
  • Copies from password managers are never kept.

Fast when you talk, gone when you stop

The models run on your Mac's GPU through MLX, Apple's framework for Apple Silicon. Navo wakes the engine the moment you press the key, so the model loads while you are still speaking. When you are done, it gives the memory back.

  • You choose the idle time: 2, 5, 10, 30 or 60 minutes.
  • Asleep, the engine takes a few dozen MB instead of several GB.
  • A speech model and a language model are never in memory together, and a language model never takes more than 6 GB.

Small things that add up

No dictation is ever lost

If transcription fails, the recording stays in your history with a Retry button.

Your own dictionary

Teach it names and terms, and set exact replacements for what it should write.

Two engines, side by side

Run one recording through two speech models and compare what each one wrote.

Progress in the menu bar

The icon fills like a pie while a long file is transcribed, then turns into a green check.

No invented words

Silent stretches are never sent to the model, so it cannot make up text for them.

Delete means delete

Recordings are erased from disk, not moved to the Trash, and deleted text is overwritten.

Your keys, your way

Dictate with Right Option, Right Command, Right Control or fn, and record your own shortcuts.

A local API

Your scripts and other apps can use the same models through an OpenAI compatible API.

100% private. Everything runs on your Mac.

No cloud, no account, no tracking. The engine listens on 127.0.0.1 only and loads its models in offline mode. The internet is used once, to download the models you choose. Turn Wi-Fi off and dictate: it still works.

  • Your voice and your text never leave the device.
  • Deleting is permanent: recordings are erased from disk and deleted text is overwritten.
  • Settings shows the proof: the device, the address, the memory in use and how long each transcription took.
  • The code is open. Read it, build it, change it.

What you need

Mac
Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported.
System
macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Recording what the Mac plays in a meeting needs macOS 14.4 or later.
Memory
8 GB at least, 16 GB recommended. With 8 GB, keep one speech model on (Whisper is the lightest). Summaries are comfortable with 16 GB.
Disk
About 8 GB free for the first setup, plus the size of each extra model, from 1.6 to 5.2 GB.
Internet
Only for the first download. After that Navo works offline.
Hugging Face token
Only for the Cohere model: a free account and a Read token. Every other model downloads without an account.
Permissions
Microphone, and Accessibility so Navo can paste the text. System Audio Recording if you record meetings.

Install and first setup

About ten minutes, most of it the one time download.

  1. Download the DMG file, open it and drag Navo into Applications.
  2. Open Navo. If macOS says it cannot check the app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Navo. You do this once.
  3. Navo opens its Settings. The Setup list shows every step and what is already on your Mac. Click Install: it downloads Python, MLX, a speech model and the small cleanup model, about 7 GB, one time. With no token it installs Audar.
  4. Optional, for Cohere. Accept the model's terms on Hugging Face, create a Read token at huggingface.co/settings/tokens, paste it in Settings and click Download next to Cohere.
  5. Optional, for summaries. Click Download Gemma in the Setup list (5.2 GB, no token).
  6. Allow Microphone and Accessibility when Navo asks.
  7. When the sidebar says Local engine: Ready, click into any text field, hold Right Option, talk, and release.

For developers: a local API

The engine is an OpenAI compatible API on http://127.0.0.1:7861. Send audio and get text, or send text to a language model, from any script or tool while Navo is running. No key is needed.

To build from source, run ./scripts/run.sh. The app is native Swift (SwiftUI and AppKit) and the engine is Python (FastAPI and MLX).

Read the API documentation

# Audio in, text out
curl -F file=@meeting.m4a -F language=ar \
  http://127.0.0.1:7861/audar/v1/audio/transcriptions
# Text in, summary out
curl http://127.0.0.1:7861/gemma/v1/chat/completions \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"messages": [
        {"role": "system", "content": "Summarize in three points."},
        {"role": "user", "content": "..."}]}'
